# Beauty of Cloud 2.0 — Finalists Guidelines

**Cloud Solution Planning & Pitching Phase**

*University of Sri Jayewardenepura IEEE Student Branch · IEEE Computer Society, University of Sri Jayewardenepura Student Branch Chapter*

---

## 1. Overview

Congratulations to all shortlisted teams! You have successfully completed Round 1 (Ideathon Phase) and have been selected for the Final Round of Beauty of Cloud 2.0.

In this final phase, teams are expected to:

- **Refine and expand the cloud architecture proposal submitted in Round 1.**
- **Prepare a clear, concise, and professional presentation.**
- **Develop and demonstrate a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP) during your assigned presentation slot.**

### Important Requirement Update

A working MVP is mandatory for the final evaluation. While a fully production-ready system is not expected, you must implement a meaningful portion of the cloud architecture and demonstrate it live (or via a high-quality recorded demonstration if technical constraints arise).

---

## 2. Key Deliverables

Every shortlisted team must prepare and submit/present the following deliverables:

- **Pitch Presentation (Pitch Deck):** A clear, visual slide deck covering problem understanding, solution architecture, security, scalability, cost management, and live demo breakdown.
- **MVP/Working Demo:** A functional demonstration showcasing the core capabilities of your cloud solution, presented during your allocated slot.

---

## 3. Presentation Guidelines

### Session Structure

| Session Component | Allocated Duration |
|---|---|
| Presentation Duration | 15 minutes |
| Q/A & Viva Session | 5 minutes |
| **Total Time Slot** | **20 minutes** |

### Recommended Slide Deck Structure

1. **Title Slide:** Team introduction & locked scenario
2. **Problem Analysis:** Core pain points & contextual scope
3. **Proposed Solution Overview:** High-level value proposition
4. **Cloud Solution Architecture:** Detailed diagram with cloud service justifications
5. **Security & Compliance Framework:** Data protection, IAM, and privacy controls
6. **Scalability, Reliability & Cost Strategy:** Resource elasticity & budget optimization
7. **Implementation Roadmap:** Development phases and future milestones
8. **Live MVP Demonstration:** Core user flow walk through
9. **Engineering Challenges & Learnings:** Key constraints and future enhancements
10. **Conclusion & Q&A**

### Presentation Tips

- Keep slides clean, highly visual, and legible. Architectural diagrams are strongly prioritized by evaluators.
- Articulate design rationale — focus on why specific cloud services were chosen rather than simply listing them.
- Ensure all team members contribute effectively during the presentation and viva session.

---

## 4. MVP & Demo Requirements

- **Scope:** The MVP does not need to cover every edge case, but it must demonstrate the core value proposition and key user flows.
- **Live Execution:** Demonstrations should ideally be conducted live during the presentation slot.
- **Fallback Option:** If live execution is limited by external APIs or credit costs, a crisp, well-narrated recorded demo combined with architectural explanation is acceptable.
- **Clarity on Scope:** Teams must explicitly communicate:
  - **Fully functional capabilities built in the MVP.**
  - **Partially integrated or mocked services.**
  - **Features deferred to future releases.**

> **Note:** Teams that present slides without any functional MVP demonstration will receive significant markdowns.

---

## 5. Final Round Judging Criteria

| Criteria | Weightage | What Evaluators Will Look For |
|---|---|---|
| Solution Architecture & Scalability | 25% | Architectural clarity, proper cloud service selection, logical justifications, and resilience under high load. |
| Problem Solving & Innovation | 20% | Direct alignment with real-world problems, original technical design, and practical feasibility. |
| Security & Compliance | 15% | Specific security mechanisms, data encryption, access governance, and domain regulatory standards. |
| Availability, Monitoring & Reliability | 15% | High availability design, fault tolerance, proactive monitoring, and disaster recovery planning. |
| Cost Management | 10% | Cost awareness, utilization of serverless/auto-scaling tiers, and budget efficiency. |
| Presentation & Documentation | 10% | Visual clarity of slides, articulation, quality of supporting documents and system diagrams. |
| MVP Demo Quality | 5% | Functional completeness of core features, live execution quality, and transparent technical reporting. |
| **Total** | **100%** | Comprehensive evaluation across all technical and presentation dimensions. |

---

## 6. Rules & Compliance

- Teams must strictly adhere to the scenario locked in during Round 1.
- Cloud platforms permitted: AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Microsoft Azure, or a well-justified multi-cloud approach.
- Teams are encouraged to utilize free tiers, sandbox environments, or student cloud credits. Excessive spending is not required or evaluated.
- Team composition must remain strictly identical to official registration.
- All registered members should be present for the final presentation and viva session.
