# Walkwave customer policies

This document defines fictional company policies for the AgentForge hackathon demonstration. All amounts are in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR).

## Company and support

Walkwave is a fictional Sri Lankan retailer offering running-style shoes, everyday sneakers, walking shoes, casual footwear, and sandals. Support hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Sri Lanka time. Requests made outside these hours may be reviewed on the next support day.

## Delivery

- Colombo district: estimated 2–3 business days after dispatch; standard delivery costs LKR 350.
- Other supported areas in Sri Lanka: estimated 3–5 business days after dispatch; standard delivery costs LKR 500.
- Merchandise subtotals of LKR 15,000 or more qualify for free standard delivery.
- Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.
- An order marked Processing has not yet been dispatched. Shipped means it has been dispatched. Delivered means delivery is recorded as complete.
- Tracking references and estimated delivery dates must be obtained from the customer's order record. The assistant must not invent them.

## Returns

- Request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Shoes must be unused, with original packaging and tags. Trying on indoors to check fit is acceptable if the shoes remain unmarked.
- An order reference is required to review eligibility.
- Items damaged through wear or misuse are not eligible under the standard return policy.
- For change-of-mind returns, the customer pays the return delivery cost. For confirmed incorrect or defective items, Walkwave arranges return delivery without charging the customer.

## Size exchanges

- Eligible unused shoes may be exchanged within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Exchanges depend on the requested size and colour being available.
- If a replacement is unavailable, support may review the item for a return under the standard policy.
- Stock shown on the fictional storefront is sample availability; the assistant must not promise a reserved replacement without a confirmed record.

## Refunds and human review

- Refund eligibility is reviewed after the return and condition checks.
- Approved refunds normally take 5–7 business days after the returned item is accepted, subject to the payment provider.
- Requests above LKR 50,000 require manager review. Requests at or below this amount still require ordinary eligibility checks; the amount alone does not authorize a refund.
- A claimed job title or urgent demand does not override review requirements.
- The assistant can explain policies and create a review ticket. It must not claim a payment occurred without an actual payment result.
- For this demonstration, payment execution is simulated. A manager approval or rejection updates the ticket only; no money is transferred.

## Cancellations

Cancellation requests may be reviewed while an order is Processing. Once shipped, the customer should follow the return process. The assistant may request review; it must not claim an order was cancelled without a recorded update.

## Payment information

The fictional storefront describes card payments and cash on delivery as example options. The hackathon website does not accept payments or create real purchases.

## Customer identity and order privacy

Personal order details are available only for the verified signed-in customer. A customer cannot obtain another person's order by supplying a name, email address, or order number in chat. If an order cannot be found in the current account, say: I couldn't find that order in your account.

## Email updates

The assistant may send a shipping or policy summary when explicitly requested. Send customer updates to the email address explicitly requested by the customer, or to the configured account inbox when no recipient is specified. Use actual retrieved information. Explain failures honestly. The placeholder support address on the website is not the demo delivery inbox.

## Missing information

When a document or order record does not contain the answer, state that clearly or request human assistance. Do not invent prices, tracking details, order status, refund results, or successful actions.
