# Walkwave

## Identity

- Company: Walkwave
- Type: fictional Sri Lankan footwear retailer
- Tagline: Every step, your way.
- Currency: Sri Lankan rupees (LKR)
- Audience: customers looking for everyday sneakers, running shoes, casual footwear, and sandals
- Brand voice: friendly, clear, practical, and welcoming
- Suggested visual direction: warm off-white background, dark charcoal text, ocean teal accents

## About copy

Walkwave makes finding your next everyday pair simple. From morning walks and busy commutes to weekend plans, our collection brings together comfortable styles, useful size choices, and straightforward delivery across Sri Lanka.

Browse the collection, check your order, or ask the Walkwave assistant about delivery, sizing, exchanges, and returns.

## Demo boundaries

This is a sample storefront, not an operating retailer. Contact information, prices, inventory, and tracking references are fictional. The website should identify Walkwave as a demo company without interrupting the customer flow.

No real checkout or payment collection is required. Product buttons can open product information or suggest a question to the assistant. Do not display successful purchase confirmations for purchases that never occurred.
