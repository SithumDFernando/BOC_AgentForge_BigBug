## Delivery and returns

- Colombo district: estimated 2–3 business days after dispatch; delivery fee LKR 350.
- Other supported areas in Sri Lanka: estimated 3–5 business days after dispatch; delivery fee LKR 500.
- Orders with a merchandise subtotal of LKR 15,000 or more receive free standard delivery.
- Eligible unused shoes can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Size exchanges are subject to stock availability.

Use `policies.md` as the source of truth. These are fictional service commitments, not live courier guarantees.

## FAQ

**How can I check my order?** Ask the assistant while signed in to your demo customer account.

**Can I exchange a size?** Yes, eligible unused items can be exchanged within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock.

**Where do you deliver?** Standard delivery is offered across supported locations in Sri Lanka. See the policy for estimated times and charges.

**Can I receive an email update?** Ask the assistant to email your order status to your account inbox or an email address you explicitly provide.

**What if my request needs a manager?** The assistant creates a review request and explains that it is awaiting a human decision.
